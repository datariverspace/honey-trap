About

Honey Trap is a three-piece band of brothers from Leeds.

We take from wherever you are to a golden sandy beach floating in space, there’s a tiki bar serving your favourite cocktail and the ocean laps gently at your feet. You smile and wave as your friends sail over the horizon towards the shore. The music begins to play and a wave of groovy rock washes over your senses. everything feels just right. Welcome to the sun-kissed abyss. It’s a place you can escape to whenever you like. And when you come to our show, we can escape together.

Our sound is alternative rock you can dance to. Sometimes rock, sometimes funk, sometimes hip-hop, always Honey Trap.

Owen — Vocals, Drums Aidan — Bass, BVs Ben — Guitar, BVs
