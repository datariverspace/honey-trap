Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what happens to your information if you use the fan upload tool at honeytrap.band/#uploader ("the Uploader"), run by Honey Trap ("we", "us").

What we collect

When you use the Uploader, we collect:

  • The photo or video files you choose to upload
  • Any optional details you type in — your name, your Instagram or TikTok handle, and a note about where or when the footage was taken

We don't use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics on this page, and we don't collect anything you haven't actively submitted.

How we use it

Files and details you submit go directly into a private Google Drive folder that only Honey Trap can access. We use them to find and enjoy fan-shot content from our shows, and we may ask your permission before reposting anything publicly (for example crediting your handle if we share a clip on Instagram).

Who can see it

Nobody outside Honey Trap. Uploads are not published automatically and are not visible to other fans or the public. We will not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

How it gets there

The Uploader uses the Google Drive API to place your files directly into our private Drive folder. Google's own privacy policy governs how Google Drive itself handles data in transit and storage — see Google's Privacy Policy for details. We don't operate any separate storage of our own; everything lives in that one Drive folder.

How long we keep it

We keep uploaded content for as long as it's useful to us — for example, footage from a specific show might be kept indefinitely if we end up using it, or deleted if it's no longer needed. You can ask us to delete anything you've submitted at any time (see Contact below).

Your choices

Submitting your name, handle, or note is entirely optional — you can upload anonymously if you prefer. If you'd like something you've already submitted removed, just get in touch.

Children's privacy

The Uploader isn't directed at children, and we ask that it not be used by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want something you submitted removed? Email us at honeytrapsounds@gmail.com.
